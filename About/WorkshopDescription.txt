[h1]Smoothing Expanded[/h1]

Make RimWorld's natural stone smoothing faster, adjust its wealth or beauty if wanted, and build smooth stone directly from raw chunks.

[h2]Source and development[/h2]
Source code, build instructions, validation tooling and component-reuse terms: [url=https://github.com/Lordlony/SmoothingExpanded]GitHub[/url]. The Workshop download contains only the files RimWorld needs.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Adjustable wall and floor smoothing speeds from 0.25x to 10x (default 2x)
[*]Optional instant smoothing
[*]Optional wealth and beauty overrides for vanilla smoothed walls and floors
[*]Optional chunk-built smooth walls and floors: one matching raw chunk per tile
[*]Choose chunk-built, genuine vanilla results, or both catalogues
[*]Sandstone, granite, limestone, slate, marble, and Odyssey Vacstone
[*]No research prerequisite; normal construction work and vanilla deconstruction returns
[/list]

[h2]Dependencies[/h2]
[b]RimWorld Core is the only requirement.[/b]

[url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2009463077]Harmony[/url] is optional. Without it, wall speed, wall wealth and chunk construction still work. Independent floor speed, floor instant mode and floor wealth require Harmony. Odyssey is optional; Vacstone appears only while it is active.

[h2]Chunk-built surfaces[/h2]
Chunk construction is disabled by default. Each tile costs one matching raw chunk and uses normal stone construction work.

[list]
[*][b]Chunk-built:[/b] deconstructible walls and removable floors.
[*][b]Vanilla:[/b] consumes the chunk and becomes a genuine vanilla smoothed wall or permanent floor.
[*][b]Both:[/b] shows both result types.
[/list]

Chunk-built surfaces follow normal material-return rules, so refund mods can control returns. Their optional wealth and beauty overrides default off. Catalogue and constructed-value changes require a restart.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3717703166]Unsmooth Surface[/url] - converts chunk-built surfaces to matching vanilla rough surfaces.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1524681306]CCP's Stone Tweaks Vanilla[/url] - Core chunk-built floors inherit its stone colours.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2568850063]Configurable Deconstruct Percentage[/url] - works through normal deconstruction.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3563882422]Better Architect Menu[/url] and Architect Menu Optimizer - supported floor catalogue path.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=872762753]MinifyEverything[/url] - supports minifying and reinstalling chunk-built walls.
[/list]

Vanilla-result construction has the broadest mod compatibility. The experimental floor-wealth option is off by default and may overlap with mods that replace wealth or raid calculations.

[h2]Important uninstall instructions[/h2]
[b]Do not remove this mod from a save containing chunk-built surfaces.[/b]

Load the save, open Smoothing Expanded settings and select [b]Prepare loaded save for uninstall[/b]. It converts completed custom surfaces, including packed walls in containers and caravans, and cancels unfinished blueprints and frames. Save after a successful conversion report, then remove the mod. Vanilla-result surfaces are already uninstall-safe.
