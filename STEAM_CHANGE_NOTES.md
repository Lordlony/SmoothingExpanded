Settings interface

- Reworked Mod Settings into compact Home, Smoothing, Vanilla Surfaces, Chunk
  Construction and Save Safety pages.
- Added highlighted active tabs, sensible per-page scrolling, scoped resets,
  clearer Harmony availability, and a guarded Reset All action.
- Kept uninstall preparation separate from normal settings. Reset All never
  converts a save.

Existing saves and settings remain compatible. Restart RimWorld after updating.
